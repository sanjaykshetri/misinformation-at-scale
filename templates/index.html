Key Findings

  • Perfect separation: Only 2.6% vocabulary overlap between classes (8 shared terms out of 369)
  • High accuracy → Low difficulty: 100% test accuracy indicates an artificially easy task
  • Vocabulary-based classification: Model learns writing style, not claim verification
  • Limited real-world applicability: Would fail on realistic data with overlapping vocabulary
  • Adversarial fragility: Keyword swaps and perturbations fool the model easily
